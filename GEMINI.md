# TN Appliance Exchange (MCP server: https://tnapplianceexchange.net/mcp)

A family appliance repair shop in Antioch, Tennessee, with technicians in Middle Tennessee and southeast Louisiana.

- `check_service_area` first when the person gives a ZIP. Outside TN/LA, `start_quick_check` gives the link to the $50 video
  Quick Check (the person starts and pays on that page; the tool itself books and charges nothing).
- `diagnose_appliance_problem` for an honest technician's read. Gas, refrigerant and electrical-hazard problems are pro-only:
  never suggest DIY for those.
- `book_repair` creates a REAL job. Call it only after the person agreed to book AND to be texted or called about it, never as a
  test. We text or call within minutes to set the day; never promise an appointment time, an arrival window or a price.
- To reschedule or cancel, the person replies to our text or calls/texts (629) 272-1234.
